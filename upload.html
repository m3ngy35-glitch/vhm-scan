To fix · ត្រូវកែ

Reads one document per scanned order (about 4,000 for a full day). A finished day is saved as a summary, so opening it again costs 1 read.

⬇ Export scans · which orders went through the manager

One row per scanned order: who scanned it, the result, the scan counts, and every manager / supervisor step (ship short approved or rejected, technical quirk, dispute, PIN approval). Reads about one document per scanned order.

Same words · ពាក្យដូចគ្នា

Colour/size words that mean the same thing in PokPok and Vrich. One group per line, separated by =, e.g. ស = សរ. Phones use the list at once; the "Orders that can't scan" list is re-checked.

💲 Price check · ពិនិត្យតម្លៃ

ON: a scanned PokPok label counts only if its PokPok Price is the same as the price of the line in the Vrich order (per piece: Vrich's Price ÷ Quantity). Wrong price = red WRONG PRICE on the phone, not counted; staff take the right item, or a supervisor approves / ⚡ quirk. Items with no price (empty or 0) are not checked, and neither are orders uploaded before this version — upload today's order file again to add the prices. Phones switch at once.

Staff & supervisors

Staff names appear as a list on the phones. A supervisor PIN approves one item in one order when the catalog cannot be fixed straight away. Use 6 digits or more.
