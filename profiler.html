Connecting…

Profiles update by themselves each time the Vrich order file is uploaded on VHM Scan's upload page (box 3). To add older history once, drop older Vrich order exports here; they are saved online too. Orders already saved are left as they are.

In VHM Live Log, open Export / Import via spreadsheet, tap Export weights, then Copy for spreadsheet. Paste it here. Pasting again replaces the old weights.
Flags any top or bottom that is 2+ sizes away from what the customer usually buys. Check with the customer before it ships.

Profiles count paid orders only (Transferred above $0). Unpaid orders are left out of spend, sizes, alerts and rankings, and show crossed out in the order history. Re-uploading a day on VHM Scan updates its payment status. Usual size is the size bought most, by quantity, and tops and bottoms are kept separate. Customers are matched by phone number, taken from the phone field or from the address when staff typed it there. Orders with no phone are matched by Facebook name. Weight is the band most of a customer's tops and bottoms agree on, using the Live Log weight for that code and size. Codes not in the Live Log use its default size guide and are marked * in Top customers. A weight the customer typed in a comment (for example 64kg) is shown first.
